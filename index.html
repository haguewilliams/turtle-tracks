Find a Turtle

01 Field notes · Habitat

Where to Look

Eastern box turtles live in woodlands, fields, and yards—especially near cover like logs, leaf litter, and brush. They often appear after rain or on warm, humid days.

What to Look For

FIELD GUIDE · COMPARE FEATURES

Eastern Box Turtle

Terrapene carolina carolina

A land-dwelling turtle with a high-domed shell, hinged plastron, sturdy legs, and varied yellow, orange, and brown markings.

Look for these clues

Check only features you can see without picking up or turning over the turtle.

Photo assessment: age & sex

A clear photo can support a cautious sex estimate, but exact age cannot reliably be read from a photograph. Turtle Tracks uses broad life stages rather than pretending to know an exact number of years.
