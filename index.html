Find a Turtle

Where to Look

Eastern box turtles live in woodlands, fields, and yards—especially near cover like logs, leaf litter, and brush. They often appear after rain or on warm, humid days.

What to Look For

little details.

Don't pick up a turtle just to identify it. Observe from the ground and photograph the features you need.

Identify a Turtle

Eastern Box Turtle

Terrapene carolina carolina

A land-dwelling turtle with a high-domed shell, hinged plastron, sturdy legs, and varied yellow, orange, and brown markings.

Eastern Box Turtle

Terrapene carolina carolina

The Eastern Box Turtle is a land-dwelling turtle known for its high-domed shell, hinged plastron (bottom shell), and varied markings.
